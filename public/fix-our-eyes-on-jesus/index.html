Fix Our Eyes on Jesus

Fix Our Eyes on Jesus
Samara Allen
Sunday, 28 September 2025

[Verse 1]
Long ago the prophets spoke,
Shadows cast and promises told,
But now the Son has made it plain,
The radiance of God’s own name.
High Priest who knows our every need,
Through His blood we are made free.

[Chorus]
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
Author of our faith,
Greater than the angels,
The cross became His place.
Seated with the Father,
Forever He will reign,
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
The Lamb for sinners slain.

[Verse 2]
By faith the ancients walked the road,
Trusting God though not yet shown.
We run the race they set before,
With Christ our joy, our great reward.
Better covenant, better way,
Hope unshaken, here to stay.

[Chorus]
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
Author of our faith,
Greater than the angels,
The cross became His place.
Seated with the Father,
Forever He will reign,
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
The Lamb for sinners slain.

[Bridge]
A kingdom unshaken,
A fire that remains,
Our God is consuming,
Forever the same.
So lift up your voices,
With boldness draw near,
For mercy is waiting,
Our Saviour is here.

[Final Chorus]
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
Our Saviour, strong and true.
The finisher of faith,
Who makes all things new.
Lifted high in glory,
Forever we will sing,
Fix our eyes on Jesus,
Our Prophet, Priest, and King.
