Uncontainable

Uncontainable
Warwick Allen
Wednesday, 16 September 2026

[Verse 1]
O Lord, king of us,
You've made so numerous
Your thoughts toward us
and deeds so wondrous.
You, Lord, are unmatched:
none can be compared!
Your wondrous acts:
more than can be declared.
May all who seek You,
who by you are awed,
rejoice, be glad in You,
and sing, “Great is the Lord!”

[Pre-chorus]
Into my life, Your Spirit's bestowed;
into my heart, Your love's been sown.

[Chorus]
This love You placed in me
is uncontainable;
my heart bursts at the seams.
Your goodness and great deeds
are incomparable;
I praise the God who redeems.

[Verse 2]
Trying to say
how my spirit soars
thinking of the goodness
of my Lord.
Trying to express
my gratitude
knowing each good gift
comes from You.
Searching for a way
to exclaim
the beauty, the wonder
of Your name.

[Pre-chorus]
In my life, Your goodness grows;
from my heart, Your goodness flows.

[Chorus]
This love You placed in me
is uncontainable;
my heart bursts at the seams.
Your goodness and great deeds
are incomparable;
I praise the God who redeems.

[Bridge]
For He who is mighty
has done great things for me,
and holy is His name.
For those who fear Him,
He has abounding mercy,
from generation to generation.

[Verse 3]
So here I offer
my humble verse,
to say how my heart
begins to burst
when thoughts of You
fill my mind
and I ponder
Your love divine,
when I remember
Your great might,
and am assured
You hold me tight.

[Pre-chorus]
In my life, You've made Your home;
from my heart, I'll make You known.

[Chorus]
This love You placed in me
is uncontainable;
my heart bursts at the seams.
Your goodness and great deeds
are incomparable;
I praise the God who redeems.
