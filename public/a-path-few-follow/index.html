A Path Few Follow

A Path Few Follow
Warwick Allen
Thursday, 17 September 2026

Can one say a saying
that's distinct from a million others?
Can one feel a feeling
that's deeper than the mask that covers?

If it's not found inside,
where can one find authenticity?
If we are wont to hide,
can we claim to know reality?

When a yes is a yes
with no shroud of ambiguity,
when no-one needs to guess,
that's when we've found our integrity.

There is a way that few dare to go;
there is a path that few here follow.
