Broken Glass

Broken Glass
Warwick Allen
Wednesday, 1 October 1997

I am given silver
But I want pewter

I have gold but yet
I yearn for brass

And quartz it seems I prefer
To my diamonds with their glitter

And all my pearls I forget
As I long for a piece of broken glass
